# Sum up 19 November 2026's world news in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty `news-image-20261119`.

The program is `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only). The image
is embedded in `main.py` as base64, and the program decodes it to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024x1024, under 1 MiB,
and names Thursday 19 November 2026.

Six stories, all 19 November 2026:
1. First-ever Trump-Xi-Putin summit, Shenzhen APEC.
2. ICJ delivers its verdict in Gambia v. Myanmar (Rohingya), The Hague.
3. Three tankers on fire after hitting mines east of the Strait of Hormuz, Iran says.
4. Israeli tanks cross the Gaza ceasefire line, central Gaza.
5. Ethiopia-Eritrea closer to all-out war, Horn of Africa.
6. M5.7 quake shakes Kumamoto, Japan; two injured, no tsunami warning.

Sources were checked on 19 November 2026, including Nikkei Asia,
Financial Express, Al Jazeera, Reuters, Al Arabiya, NDTV, IRNA,
Japan Times and Kyodo News. No verdict outcome, no summit outcome,
no ship names and no casualty figures are stated in the image.
